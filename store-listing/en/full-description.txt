Every public holiday in your Spanish region in a clear calendar, with the year's long weekends and a planner to make the most of your annual leave.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See national, regional and local holidays for each month, in different colours.
• Find out how many days are left until the next holiday.
• Mark your town's local holidays by tapping the day.
• See the long weekends: holidays on a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday and which days to take off.
• Count the working days between two dates, excluding weekends and holidays.
• See how many working days the month and the year have.
• Choose any region: Andalusia, Aragon, Asturias, Balearic Islands, Canary Islands, Cantabria, Castilla-La Mancha, Castile and León, Catalonia, Ceuta, Valencian Community, Extremadura, Galicia, Community of Madrid, Melilla, Region of Murcia, Navarre, Basque Country and La Rioja.
• Use it offline.

HOLIDAY PLANNER
Tell us how many days of leave you have and we suggest when to take them to link holidays and weekends and get the most consecutive days off. You will see how many days you use and how many you rest in each block.

PREMIUM
Premium removes all ads and keeps the holiday planner always unlocked. Choose a monthly subscription or a one-off lifetime payment. You can also unlock the planner for free for 24 hours by watching an ad.

DISCLAIMER
Holidays are calculated using each region's rules. Some regions move or change holidays every year, so always check the official calendar published in the Spanish Official Gazette (BOE), your region's gazette and your town hall. This is an independent app and is not affiliated with any public administration.
